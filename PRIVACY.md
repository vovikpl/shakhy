# Privacy Policy — Pair Play

_Last updated: November 15, 2026_

Pair Play is a collection of games for two players. It is made by an individual developer.

**What the app collects:** nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
It does not send any personal data to the developer or to third parties.

**Player name:** the name you type is stored only on your phone. When you play with a friend,
it is sent directly to your friend's phone so it can be shown during the game.

**Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and location permissions:** they are used only to find and connect to a friend's
phone nearby or on the same Wi-Fi network. Android requires the location permission for Bluetooth
search on some phones; the app never reads or stores your location.

**Children:** the app is suitable for children and collects no data from anyone.
