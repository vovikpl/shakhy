**Full description:**
Play together on one phone or on two phones side by side, no internet needed!

6 games for two players:
♞ Chess with all the rules, undo and draw offers
⚓ Sea Battle: place your fleet and sink your opponent's ships
🏒 Air Hockey: split screen, one mallet each, or play against the computer
🎯 Tanks: arcade tank battles
🏎️ Racing on three tracks
🗺️ Maze Quest: a co-op adventure with keys, buttons and colored paths

How to play:
• 2 players on one phone
• Two phones nearby via Bluetooth, no Wi-Fi or internet
• Two phones on the same Wi-Fi network
• Against the computer in most games

No accounts, no data collection. Great for families, friends, siblings and road trips.
